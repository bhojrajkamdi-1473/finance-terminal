Learning finance through work.

( 02 ) Analytical framework

Observe, question, verify, falsify.

How I approach any problem in finance — research, analysis or audit. A personal framework, not an institutional methodology.

01Observe→ 02Question→ 03Hypothesise→ 04Connect→ 05Verify→ 06Judge→ 07Project→ 08Falsify
01 · 02

Observe & Question

Start with the data and the situation as they are. Question what is assumed, what is stated, and what is missing.

03 · 04 · 05

Hypothesise, Connect & Verify

Form a testable hypothesis, connect it to available evidence, then verify against the source rather than the summary.

06 · 07 · 08

Judge, Project & Falsify

Reach a judgment, project its consequences, then actively look for the evidence that would prove it wrong.

( 03 ) Audit thinking

The discipline of checking things properly.

A practical personal approach developed through audit internship work — not an official auditing standard.

01Understand→ 02Identify→ 03Verify→ 04Trace→ 05Document→ 06Conclude
01 · 02

Understand & Identify

Map the business and its transactions first; then identify what actually needs to be tested and where risk concentrates.

03 · 04

Verify & Trace

Verify a transaction against its supporting documentation, and trace it from entry to ledger to financial statement.

05 · 06

Document & Conclude

Document what was checked so the work can be reviewed later — and state the limits of what was tested.

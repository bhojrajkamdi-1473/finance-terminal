Equity Research, IPO & Financial Statement Analysis

Featured Academic live projects Equity Research

Question

How do IPO fundamentals translate into sustained secondary-market performance — and what does a long-horizon financial-statement read reveal about quality?

Approach

Three separate company studies, each combining fundamental review with ratio and technical analysis — guided live projects, not internship work.

Finding

Valuation discipline matters at listing; operating resilience shows up over years in margins, leverage and cash conversion. Detail per study below.

Fundamental review plus RSI, MACD, moving averages and volume work from listing to 31 October 2025. 02

Private Equity Fund Accounting & Hedge Fund Structure

Featured Academic class exercises Investment operations

Question

How do private-equity and hedge-fund structures actually operate — capital calls, fees, waterfalls, incentive allocation and NAV?

Approach

Excel-based class exercises: a 16-investor PE fund ($1.54M commitment, 3% management fee), the Pearl Masters hedge-fund structure ($5M raised, 4% fee, 20% incentive over a 6% hurdle), and a 13-investor rebalancing model ($22.94M, 10% incentive with high-water mark).

Finding

Working mechanics: an 8% preferred-return waterfall, quarterly management-fee accrual, incentive crystallisation, and NAV computation under different scenarios ($1.53M and $3.0M). Evidence is the three workbooks; no standalone written report was produced.

Corporate Actions & Financial Impact — Jindal Worldwide (4:1 Bonus)

Featured Academic live project Capital markets

Question

What did Jindal Worldwide's 2025 bonus issue do to shareholder wealth, capital structure, EPS and market valuation?

Approach

Case study of the 4:1 issue (record date 28 Feb 2025): bonus mechanics, reserve capitalisation, EPS impact and the pre/post-bonus price path. Guide: Prof. Ajit Shah.

Finding

Share capital ₹20.05 Cr → ₹100.26 Cr; EPS ₹0.92 → ₹0.14 (−84.8%); pre-bonus peak ₹471.20 adjusted to ₹94.24, with the price at ₹25.50 by April 2026 — liquidity improved, dilution severe, price weak.

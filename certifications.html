( 01 ) How this page is governed

The standard behind every listed credential.

  • Every entry states its exact status — pass, participation, simulation, course or test score.
  • Certifications, courses and simulations are never mixed; each stays in its own category.
  • Unfinished work is never shown as completed.
  • Where a claim on an older resume exceeds the certificate — such as a grade for a participation-only programme — this site publishes only what the certificate supports.

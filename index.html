05
Capital Markets · Jindal Worldwide Ltd.

Corporate Actions — Financial Impact

Academic project · Not investment advice

What was the financial impact of the 4:1 bonus issue? Traced through announcement, record date (28 February 2025) and entitlement mechanics, then measured on capital and per-share figures.

Finding — 4:1 bonus issue capitalised ₹80.21 crore of reserves into share capital; EPS declined 84.8% and BVPS ~78% on the expanded share count.

Why it matters — demonstrates the ability to translate a corporate action into changes in capital structure and per-share financial metrics.

View report↗
What was the financial impact of the 4:1 bonus issue on shareholder wealth, capital structure, EPS and market valuation?
Bonus mechanics — 4:1 ratio, record date 28 February 2025 — followed through share capital transformation, reserve capitalisation and EPS measurement against market price movement.
Share capital moved from ₹20.05 Cr to ₹100.26 Cr on reserve capitalisation of ₹80.21 Cr; EPS adjusted from ₹0.92 to ₹0.14 (an 84.8% decline) following the 4:1 issuance, with BVPS declining ~78% and ROE and capital structure analysed through the issue — figures as reported in the analysis.
Single-company study; bonus effects not isolated from broader market movement; no peer bonus-issue comparison. Output: impact analysis with signed report version. Academic project — not investment advice.
